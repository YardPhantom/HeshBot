## 7. Public prices or "contact for a quote"?

**Show prices publicly**, as "from" prices with a note that a quote confirms scope. Reasons:
- The closest local competitors all publish prices: Blogrator (Nanaimo, "flat, published prices"), Elliot Digital ("published here so you can plan before we talk"), Long Technology, CurbChat, PowOne, and every SaaS tool above. A hidden price looks expensive next to them.
- Café, dental, and trades owners self-qualify by price. A $599 / $39 figure is small enough to decide on without a meeting, and "contact for a quote" usually signals agency pricing ($1,200+).
- Keep "contact us" for the booking tier, multi-location clients, and custom integrations.

## 8. Three key findings
1. **Local price floor has collapsed:** BC options now include CurbChat at $30/mo with no setup fee and Long Technology at $199 + $20/mo. HeshBot has to sell the hand-built, local, persona'd service, not just "a chatbot".
2. **Comparable done-for-you work sits at $1,200–$1,500 setup** with $49–$249/mo care (Elliot Digital, PowOne). That leaves room for $599 setup and makes $29/mo care look underpriced. $39 is still below the $45–$99 local web-care range.
3. **Usage-priced SaaS is the hidden cost buyers fear:** Intercom and ChatBot.com charge $0.99 per AI resolution and Botpress $0.50–0.65 per conversation. HeshBot's flat care fee plus the client's own capped AI key (about $5–15/mo by Long Tech's estimate) is a real selling point. Say so on the page.
