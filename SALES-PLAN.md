# HeshBot Sales Plan (draft, internal, not for publishing)

**Product:** HeshBot, Trevor Heshka's self-hosted website chatbot (PHP, MIT, github.com/YardPhantom/HeshBot), sold as setup + monthly hosting/care. Public wording calls the model **HeshLLM:20B**. *Internal: backend choice still to be confirmed (plan text says Gemini; check commercial terms/quotas before client use, per-client API keys).*
**Target customers:** Nanaimo-area small businesses with FAQ-heavy sites (cafés, dental, trades).
**Rules:** free tools only. Trevor approves every post, email, and any spending first.

## Market check (Oct 2026)
- Small-business chatbot: $500–1,000 setup + $299–499/mo (BuiltABot). FAQ/lead-capture bot: $800–2,000 setup, $200–500/mo (Nicherly). Website chat agent: $1,000 setup, from $150/mo (AZE IT).
- Budget: $197–$497 one-time (Blogrator); managed from $10/mo (Botlery). Agencies: $2k–$5k + $300–$800/mo (PrimeDev).

## Offer & pricing (CAD)
| Tier | What they get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Essential** | Setup customized to the client's needs (install, custom name/persona, knowledge base from their most-asked questions, branding) + **1 year of support** | **$999** |
| **Advanced** | Fast, customized decision-making model with advanced features: booking, lead capture and custom requests | **From $2,500** |
| **Enterprise** | Complete network setup, custom AI LLM models built to the business's needs, data kept protected locally, plus Trevor's wider IT support, network & cybersecurity, and full-stack web/app development services | **Contact for pricing** |

**Monthly hosting add-on:** Website + HeshBot hosting, **$40/month** (we host the client's website and their HeshBot).

After year 1, support renewal TBD. Free DIY (MIT repo) stays available at $0. Client pays its own AI usage (cloud backends).

## KPIs
Demo page live on trevorheshka.com · 20 qualified local prospects listed · first paying client by day 60 · 3 paying clients (Essential or above) by day 90.

## 30 / 60 / 90 days
- **Days 1–30:** Demo HeshBot for a sample local business; service page (what it does, what's included, session-only chat privacy, tiers); blog post 002-heshbot (after approval); Chief of Staff builds the 20-prospect list; confirm backend terms.
- **Days 31–60:** approved outreach emails and in-person visits (Nanaimo Chamber, local Facebook groups); standardize install (knowledge build script, config, rate limits, handoff doc).
- **Days 61–90:** Upsell Essential clients to Advanced (booking/lead capture); pitch Enterprise to larger prospects.

## Who does what
- **TrevorHeshka.com:** service page, demo, case studies, HeshBot on the site itself.
- **Facebook / Instagram:** local-business posts and short demo clips, each approved first.
- **AI Search Visibility:** make the service page answerable by AI search (FAQ schema, clear local wording).
- **Chief of Staff:** prospect list, outreach drafts, follow-up reminders, proposals. Drafts only, never sent unapproved.
- **Lisa O / Youtube:** one demo video, reused on site and socials.

## Risks
Backend quotas/terms may not suit client production use · wrong AI answers: keep knowledge narrow, add fallback contact · support load: cap included changes.
